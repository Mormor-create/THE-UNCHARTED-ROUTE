Category Guidelines

Keep names clear. Help readers understand what they'll find.

Keep slugs simple. Use lowercase words separated by hyphens.

Optimize for search. Add useful metadata and descriptions.

Use featured sparingly. Highlight your most important themes.

Before creating

Make sure another category does not already cover the same topic. Each category should have a distinct purpose.
